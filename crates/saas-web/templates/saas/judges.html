For judges · about 10 minutes

Who is this agent, who answers for it, and where did the money go?

Agentrix turns a small business's website into a team of AI agents. Each agent can be identified by anyone, answers to a human with a passkey, and keeps a PayPal wallet record anyone can check. Autonomous inside the authority the owner signs; every dollar booked in Zavora ERA (hosted).
