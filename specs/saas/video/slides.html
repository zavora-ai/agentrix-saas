Each site is an OpenID Connect issuer for its agents. The code is upstream in adk-rust (zavora-ai/adk-rust#1362, merged). The cloud test objects were removed after the checks.
A human answers for it

The owner's passkey, at three moments only

1Link PayPalOnce. The agents never hold the PayPal secret; access is issued per call.
2Sign the mandateHow much an agent may pay, per payment and per day, and to whom.
3Step upA new payee, or an amount above the mandate, waits for the owner.
The signature covers exactly what is shown. In our attack suite the founder approved USD 800 and the agent then asked for USD 750: the edit was held as a new call and the approval was void. Neither paid.
